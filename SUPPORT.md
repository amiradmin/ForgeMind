# Support and Community Communication

Choose the channel that best matches your message:

- **Bug or reproducible problem:** open a Bug Report issue.
- **Feature proposal:** open a Feature Request issue.
- **Collaboration proposal:** open a Collaboration Proposal issue.
- **Question, architecture discussion, industrial use case, or introduction:** use GitHub Discussions.
- **Security vulnerability:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not open a public issue.

## Collaboration

ForgeMind welcomes collaboration with:

- Industrial and maintenance engineers
- Python, Django, FastAPI, and React developers
- Data and telemetry engineers
- Machine Learning and Computer Vision engineers
- Researchers working on Predictive Maintenance, RUL, Digital Twins, OEE, and reliability
- Technical writers and open-source community builders

When proposing collaboration, describe the problem, the value for industrial users, your relevant experience, and the smallest useful first milestone.

English and Persian messages are both welcome.

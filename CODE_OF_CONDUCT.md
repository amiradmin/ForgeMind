# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ForgeMind is committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, nationality, language, or location.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive
- Critique ideas and code, not people
- Share context and evidence when disagreeing
- Welcome newcomers and help them contribute successfully
- Respect confidentiality, intellectual property, and personal boundaries
- Keep technical discussions relevant and professional

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information
- Spam, deceptive promotion, or repeated off-topic contact
- Sharing credentials, confidential industrial data, or proprietary client material
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or project work

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the professional contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive incident details in a public issue.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially representing the ForgeMind project in public spaces.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by established open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant.
